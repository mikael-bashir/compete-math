Proof-carrying shaders

Water and cloud renderers whose step sizes, error bounds and culling radii come from theorems checked in Lean. Each page has a benchmark button; the numbers in the top right are live frames per second.

  • Certified Ocean48 cosines

    48 cosine waves, marched with step sizes proved safe in Lean (cos_lip, safe_step, safe_step_back, H_rise).

  • Certified Ocean, horizon table48 cosines

    The same sea with a per-azimuth horizon table that skips most of the marching. Exact: no missed surface, same image.

  • Poisson Ocean12 waves

    Twelve Poisson-kernel waves. Each is one cosine and one division and holds infinitely many harmonics. Lipschitz constant proved in Lean.

  • Cosine Series Ocean12 waves

    The same twelve waves written as a 16-harmonic cosine series. The baseline the Poisson version is timed against.

  • Skewed Poisson Ocean12 waves

    Poisson waves with a skew term: steep front, gentle back. Range and Lipschitz constant proved in Lean.

  • Proved Cosine Ocean12 waves

    The Poisson sea with the hardware cos replaced by a polynomial whose error is proved in Lean. Same picture, slower on this GPU.

  • Gaussian Cloud48 blobs

    48 Gaussian blobs with closed-form optical depth, no ray marching. Cheap erf with a Lean-proved error bound and a proved culling radius.
